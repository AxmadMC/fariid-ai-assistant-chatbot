## Projects and tools Ahmed has built
Ahmed's free tools are listed on the Home page under Featured Projects and on the Portfolio page: Fariid (this AI assistant), Antibot Email Shield, and Amaan Inbox (beta).

## Antibot Email Shield
Antibot Email Shield is a free WordPress plugin that keeps email addresses away from spam bots. The email is never written into the page: visitors click "Show email address", pass a quick Cloudflare Turnstile check, and the email appears for a short time before hiding again. Its setup guide is at docs.ahmedmc.com/email-shield.

## Why Ahmed made Antibot Email Shield
Ahmed built it to protect the email addresses on his own website from bots that collect them for spam, without making it harder for real people to contact him. He made it free so other website owners can protect theirs too.

## Fariid - AI Assistant
Fariid is the AI assistant chatbot on ahmedmc.com. It answers questions about Ahmed, his projects and the website using only the site's content, and links to the page each answer comes from. It runs on Cloudflare Workers, uses the Gemini API and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and is protected with Cloudflare Turnstile and rate limits.

## Why Ahmed built Fariid, this AI assistant chatbot
Ahmed built Fariid so visitors can find answers quickly without searching through pages, and to show how to build AI tools that are safe and practical. It is designed so others can reuse it on their own websites.

## Amaan Inbox (beta)
Amaan Inbox is a secure WordPress contact-form plugin, currently in beta, with a private inbox in the WordPress dashboard. The form is added with a shortcode and protected with Cloudflare Turnstile, a hidden spam trap and rate limits. Site owners can track every message and reply by email, and visitors' IP addresses are stored only as hashes to protect their data. A download is coming soon.

## Why Ahmed built Amaan Inbox
Most contact forms only forward messages to an email inbox, where they're easy to miss or lose. Ahmed wanted every message kept securely in one place on his site, where he can track and reply to it, while protecting visitors' data.
