## Why Ahmed created this website (ahmedmc.com) #about
Ahmed MC created ahmedmc.com to share the free tools he builds, show his projects, and make it easy to get in touch with him.

## Main menu of the website #menu
The top menu of ahmedmc.com has: Home (about Ahmed and his featured projects), Portfolio (his free tools, with what each one does and why he built it), Docs (step-by-step guides, starting with Antibot Email Shield), Learn, Blog, and Contact (the contact form at ahmedmc.com/contact).

## Home page #home
The Home page introduces Ahmed, shows an About section, Featured Projects (Antibot Email Shield, Fariid and Amaan Inbox), a Let's Connect section with links to LinkedIn, the Portfolio and the Contact page, and a newsletter sign-up for one short email when something new is ready.

## Footer links and social media #footer
At the bottom of every page there are links to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, plus Ahmed's social profiles: LinkedIn, GitHub, Facebook, Instagram and X.

## Finding documentation #docs
Documentation for Ahmed's projects is under Docs in the top menu. Guides for each project are on the docs site: Antibot Email Shield at docs.ahmedmc.com/email-shield, the Amaan Inbox contact form at docs.ahmedmc.com/contact-form, and the Fariid AI assistant chatbot at docs.ahmedmc.com/ai-assistant. Each guide walks through installation, setup, settings and support step by step.

## About this chat assistant and privacy #assistant
Fariid is the AI assistant on ahmedmc.com. Questions typed into the chat, plus up to four earlier messages from the same chat, are sent to Google Gemini to write the answer. The website does not store conversations; the chat disappears when the page is refreshed or closed. Because the assistant uses Google's free Gemini service, Google may use what visitors type to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it, so visitors should not share personal, sensitive or confidential information. To prevent abuse there is a daily question limit, counted with a scrambled (hashed) version of the visitor's IP address that is deleted within two days. The assistant is for adults 18 and over, and it is not available in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom. AI answers can be wrong and are not professional advice. The Privacy Policy is at ahmedmc.com/privacy and the Terms of Service at ahmedmc.com/terms.
