## How to contact Ahmed
The best way to contact Ahmed MC is through the contact form on the Contact page at ahmedmc.com/contact, or by messaging him on LinkedIn. Email addresses on the site are protected from spam bots and appear only after clicking a "Show email address" button.

## Response time
Ahmed does his best to reply as soon as he can, but replies through the contact form can take up to a week. For a faster reply, message him on LinkedIn.

## Location: where Ahmed is based
Ahmed is based in the United States.

## Reporting a bug in Antibot Email Shield
Bugs and security issues in Antibot Email Shield should be reported with the "Show bug report email" button in the Support section of the documentation at docs.ahmedmc.com/email-shield. Include what happened, the steps to repeat it, the WordPress and plugin versions, and a screenshot if possible.

## Collaboration
Ahmed is always glad to connect with people working in web development, IT, AI engineering and open source.
