## Who is Ahmed MC
Ahmed MC is the short name of Ahmed Muse Ali, an IT support specialist and web developer based in the United States. His website is ahmedmc.com and his motto is "Let's make it work."

## Current role and career goal
Ahmed currently works as a Customs Entry Writer at DHL Express. He is looking for IT support and web development roles.

## Education and certifications
Ahmed holds a bachelor's degree in Community Development. He is studying Computer Science at Phoenix College and plans to transfer to Arizona State University (ASU) for Software Engineering. He is also studying AI Engineering through CodePath. He holds the Google IT Support certificate and is preparing for the CompTIA A+ and AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certifications.

## IT support experience
Ahmed has 2+ years of IT support experience as a Technical Support Assistant at a training college and an ICT Teacher Assistant at a high school, both in Nairobi, Kenya. He supported 500+ students, 25+ teachers and 7+ staff members with hardware, software, network and account issues on Windows, macOS and Linux. He handled about 15 tickets a day using Zendesk and a custom ticketing system he built, and he set up and managed Moodle LMS for 950+ users across three schools, including two as a volunteer.

## Why Ahmed builds free tools
Ahmed is an open-source enthusiast. He builds tools in his spare time to solve problems he has faced, and he makes them free for everyone to use. His aim is to automate repetitive tasks with AI and practical web tools.
